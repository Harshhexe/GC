# Brag Plan: GC — full product tour

## What is this app?

GC is a group chat with an AI living inside it: it recaps what you missed,
runs a "Tea" mode that reports on the session afterwards, hands out daily
nicknames and weekly awards, takes slash commands, and ships a daily word game.

## The angle

**One chat app, a dozen things happening inside it.**

The previous cut was a single-feature brag about awards. This is a tour: enter
a code, land in your chats, and watch feature after feature surface out of the
same conversation. The through-line is that none of it is a separate app —
every feature lives inside the chat.

The voice stays deadpan. The app's own output is still the funniest material in
it, so the roast lines carry the open and the close.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

Unchanged, because it works: a cold roast with no logo and no setup.

> "Zero messages sent, just pure read-receipt energy."

## Key moments (the middle)

The tour, in the order a real person meets it:

- **Join with a code** — six characters, and you're in
- **Your chats** — the GC list
- **The chat itself** — real messages
- **Slash commands** — `/` opens the whole toolbox inside the composer
- **@gc** — ask the chat a question, get an answer with receipts
- **What Did I Miss** — a recap with real stat tiles
- **Tea mode** — a live session that files a report afterwards
- **Wordy** — the daily word game, scoreboard shared with the group
- **Daily names** — everyone renamed at midnight
- **The awards podium** — gold, silver, bronze

## Outro / punchline

> "Say nothing. Get named anyway."

Then the GC wordmark.

## User flow worth showing

entry → key action → result, and the tour follows it literally:

1. **Entry** — type an invite code, join a GC
2. **Key action** — talk, or use one of the commands
3. **Result** — the AI reports back: recaps, tea reports, names, awards

## Tone

- **Preset:** `default`
- **Creative direction:** a feature tour that never raises its voice
- **Interpretation:** composed, confident pacing. Each feature gets one clean
  beat and a short label. Copy stays dry and specific; no exclamation marks, no
  "and that's not all". Comedy comes only from the app's real output.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 34s

**Deliberate deviation from the 15-25s creative law.** The law exists so a brag
does not overstay; here the ask is explicitly a full tour of ten features. At
25s each feature would get roughly 2s, which breaks the readability law (a
short label needs ~0.8s settled). 34s is the shortest length that shows the
requested feature set and still lets every label be read. The alternative was
cutting half the features, which is not what was asked for.

## Audio direction

- **Role:** warm corporate bed, played straight
- **Music:** `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`, from 3.0s
- **Music treatment:** moderate throughout so labels read; small lift into the
  podium; fade to zero across the final 1.2s so the punchline lands quiet
- **Music cue guidance:** 120.19 BPM, 0.5s beat grid from 3.02s. Beat grid
  suits the sequential row/chip/card arrivals. Cue timings are guidance only —
  readability wins over any lock.
- **SFX posture:** sparse. One cue per feature arrival at most, and only where
  something actually lands.
- **Restraint rule:** no whoosh per element, no riser, no stinger on the wordmark.
