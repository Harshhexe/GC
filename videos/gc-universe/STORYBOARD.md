## Video direction

Use the adopted GC palette by role: near-black canvas, cyan signal-light, amber for action and awards, purple for identity, and rose for urgency. Display type is compact, high-impact; UI labels stay small and precise. Motion is decisive and cinematic—smooth long-tail landings, beat-synced reveals, and a quiet final hold. Each visual is introduced progressively across its time window; no front-loaded feature walls. Frames 1, 5, and 10 receive held reads. Never use browser chrome, generic AI gradients, floating screensaver motion, or unrelated stock imagery.
