Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Last updated & published: 22 August 2026

Zero-Tolerance Policy: GC strictly and unequivocally prohibits Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE) in any form. Any violation results in immediate, permanent account termination, content removal, and reporting to law enforcement and child protection authorities.

1. Purpose and Scope

GC ("the app", "our platform") is dedicated to providing a safe communication environment. We maintain a zero-tolerance standard against any content, behavior, or communication that exploits, endangers, or harms children. This policy applies to all users, group conversations, media attachments, direct communications, and profile data across the platform.

2. Prohibited Content and Conduct

The following activities are strictly forbidden on GC:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM): Creating, uploading, transmitting, possessing, linking to, or facilitating access to any visual depiction (including AI-generated, synthetic, photographic, video, or illustrated imagery) of child sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE): Any activity involving the sexual abuse, sexualization, or exploitation of a minor (under the age of 18).
  • Online Child Sexual Solicitation & Grooming: Contacting, enticing, coercing, or communicating with a minor for sexual purposes or to obtain sexually explicit content.
  • Sextortion & Coercion: Blackmailing, threatening, or coercing a child or user using private or sensitive imagery.
  • Child Trafficking & Non-Consensual Imagery: Facilitating or promoting the exploitation, trafficking, or illegal distribution of materials involving children.

3. Reporting Mechanisms

Users and moderators have clear and immediate avenues to report suspected CSAE/CSAM:

A. Direct In-App Moderation & Reporting

  • Group Admins & Moderators: Can instantly delete offending messages for everyone and remove offenders from groups.
  • Message Deletion: Any user can immediately hide or delete objectionable content from their view.

B. Dedicated Child Safety Reporting Channel

To report any instance of CSAM, CSAE, child endangerment, or grooming on GC, contact our safety team immediately:

📧 Safety & Abuse Contact: hdhiman0302@gmail.com
Reports sent to this address are treated with the highest priority and investigated within 24 hours.

4. Enforcement and Response Protocols

Upon receiving a report or discovering suspected CSAE/CSAM content, GC takes the following immediate actions:

  1. Immediate Content Removal: The reported content is immediately removed from access across all channels and servers.
  2. Permanent Account Termination: The associated user account is banned permanently. Associated authentication credentials, active sessions, and access tokens are immediately revoked.
  3. Evidence Preservation: Digital evidence (such as timestamps, IP addresses, and metadata) is preserved in accordance with applicable laws to facilitate official law enforcement investigations.

5. Mandatory Reporting to Authorities & NCMEC

In compliance with federal and international laws, GC actively cooperates with child protection organizations and law enforcement agencies worldwide:

  • National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC): When CSAM/CSAE is detected or reported, reports are submitted to the NCMEC CyberTipline at report.cybertip.org (or 1-800-843-5678).
  • Law Enforcement Referral: We cooperate fully with local, national, and international law enforcement agencies regarding investigations into child safety violations.

6. External Support Resources

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger or needs support regarding child sexual exploitation:

  • United States: National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) — missingkids.org | 1-800-843-5678
  • United Kingdom: Internet Watch Foundation (IWF) — iwf.org.uk | Childline: 0800 1111
  • International: INHOPE Global Hotline Network — inhope.org
