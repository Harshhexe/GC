Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

GC (“we”, “our”, “the app”) is a private group-chat app. This page explains what data GC collects, why, and how you can remove it.

GC is built and operated by an individual developer, not a company. If anything here is unclear, email hdhiman0302@gmail.com and you’ll get a real reply.

What we collect

Your account
Email address, password
Creates and signs in to your account (Supabase Auth). Your password is hashed, so we never see or store the plain text.
Display name, username, avatar
Shown to people in your groups so they know who’s talking.
What you send
Messages, photos, videos, voice notes, files, stickers, polls
The content you send is stored so your groups can read it and so it syncs across your devices. It is visible only to members of the group you sent it in.
Private comments
A one-to-one note attached to a message. Visible only to you and the one other person in that thread, not to the rest of the group, and not even to group admins.
Your device
Push notification token
Lets your phone or browser receive a notification when someone messages you. Apple, Google, or your browser vendor relays the notification on our behalf, and we don’t control what they see of that relay.
Microphone access
Only while you’re actively recording a voice note. GC does not listen in the background.
Camera and photo library access
Only when you choose to attach a photo or video to a message.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, or browsing history, and GC has no advertising or analytics SDKs of any kind. Nothing in this app is tracking you for marketing purposes.

AI features

Optional features (like “What did I miss?” recaps or the GC AI assistant) send the relevant chat text to a third-party AI provider, currently Anthropic or Google, to generate a response. Only what’s needed for that specific request is sent, and only when you use the feature. It is not sent continuously in the background. These providers process the text to return a result and do not use it to serve you ads.

Who can see your data

  • Messages and media: the members of the group you sent them in, and no one else.
  • Private comments: only you and the other participant in that specific thread.
  • Profile info (name, username, avatar): anyone you share a group with.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Where it’s stored

GC is built on Supabase, which stores data on servers operated by Supabase’s cloud infrastructure providers. Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).

Your controls

  • Edit or delete any message you sent, at any time.
  • Leave any group, which stops you receiving its messages.
  • Delete your account from Profile › Settings, which removes your profile and account access. Messages you already sent inside a shared group remain visible to that group, the same way a text message stays in someone else’s inbox after you delete your copy. Deleting your account does not reach into other people’s conversations.
  • Revoke microphone, camera, or notification permission at any time from your device’s system settings.

Children and child safety (CSAE policy)

GC is not directed at children under 13, and we maintain a strict zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE). Please read our published Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation for full details on our policies, reporting channels, enforcement, and cooperation with NCMEC.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the “Last updated” date above will change and, where practical, we’ll say so in the app.
