📱 How to delete your account inside the GC App

If you have the GC app installed on your iPhone or Android device, you can delete your account at any time:

  1. Open the GC App on your device.
  2. Tap your Profile picture / Avatar in the top-right corner to open Settings.
  3. Scroll down to the bottom of the Settings screen.
  4. Tap "Delete Account Permanently".
  5. Confirm deletion when prompted. Your account is deleted immediately.
